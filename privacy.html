Privacy Policy

Kit Family Assistant. Last updated October 18, 2026.

Who uses this app

Kit Family Assistant is a private app used only by one household (the Bryant family). It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app reads and updates the family's Google Calendar, reads and sends email from its own Gmail account, and reads and edits its own Google Sheets, Docs and Drive files.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the household's own features: schedule briefs, calendar updates, school-email summaries and similar reminders. It is processed on the household's own computer and with the AI service the household uses to run the assistant.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside the household except as needed to run the assistant.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

The app's only users are members of the household, who manage it directly.
